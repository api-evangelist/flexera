Ask almost any IT leader how they feel about compliance and you will hear a familiar sigh: it is an exhausting, endless cycle of audit prep, messy spreadsheets and scrambling to show that corporate policies exist on paper. Yet managing IT compliance is not getting harder simply because lawmakers are writing more rules. The real issue is that the technology beneath those rules is changing just as fast as the legal text.
