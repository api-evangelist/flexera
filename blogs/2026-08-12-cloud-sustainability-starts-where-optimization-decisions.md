A shift-left mindset for cloud sustainability means moving environmental impact closer to the decisions that shape cloud usage. In FinOps, shift left means bringing cost awareness into architecture, provisioning, and optimization decisions before inefficient usage becomes embedded in the environment. The same mindset can apply to sustainability.
