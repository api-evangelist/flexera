AI cost management is the practice of tracking, allocating, optimizing and governing every dollar an organization spends on AI. That spend hides in more places than most teams expect. Some of it sits in standalone AI apps and AI agents, plus AI add-ons inside SaaS suites, often licensed per user and sometimes bought without IT’s knowledge.
