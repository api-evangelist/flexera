Track carbon, water, and electricity across AI models, workloads and tokens The new AI Sustainability experience in the Cloud Sustainability dashboard reveals the financial and environmental impact of AI services running in connected AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud accounts. It helps organizations understand not only AI costs, but also the environmental impact associated with that consumption. Feature: View spend, carbon emissions, electricity consumption, and water usage side by side for AI services appearing on connected cloud-provider bills.
